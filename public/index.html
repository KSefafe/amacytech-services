GOOD TO KNOW

Before we meet.

Can you help me today?+

Same-day help is available when there’s an open slot. Send a WhatsApp with the issue and your location so we can confirm a time before you head over.

Where do we meet?+

We can arrange to meet on VUT campus at the Library or P-Block, or at a nearby student residence in Vanderbijlpark. Confirm the exact location on WhatsApp first.

What should I bring or prepare?+

Bring your device and charger, and back up important files before a reset or reinstall. Have your own account details ready; you can enter passwords yourself. Paid software requires a valid licence.

How does “no fix, no pay” work?+

If we can’t resolve the tech issue agreed for the session, you don’t pay for that fix. We’ll discuss the issue, scope and price before starting. Website projects are quoted separately.

Do you build websites and web apps?+

Yes to websites — business sites and portfolios are R1,000–R1,500, depending on the agreed scope. Web app development is planned for the future and isn’t available to book yet.

Is AmacyTech part of VUT?+

AmacyTech Services is an independent, student-run business. VUT is our local campus community; we are not an official university IT department.
